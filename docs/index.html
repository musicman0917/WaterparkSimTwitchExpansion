🌊 Waterpark Chaos Commands

Watching the stream earns you points automatically. Spend them with !buy <action> to mess with the streamer's waterpark - or with the streamer themself.

How it works: just being active in chat earns points over time (no need to do anything special). Once you've got enough, type !buy yeet, !buy poop, etc. in chat to spend them. Costs below are this mod's defaults - your streamer may have changed them, and !balance always shows your current total.

Chaos commands

🚀 !buy yeet 100 pts

Launches a random guest who's in view of the camera into the air.

💩 !buy poop 150 pts

Drops poop above a random pool in the park.

🌊 !buy break 300 pts

Sabotages a random waterslide.

🤸 !buy ragdoll 200 pts

Flings the streamer's own character around with a random impulse.

🤮 !buy vomitNew 150 pts

Makes a random visible guest throw up.

💦 !buy peeNew 120 pts

Makes a random visible guest pee.

🗑️ !buy trashNew 100 pts

Makes a random visible guest litter.

🔄 !buy invert 250 pts

Flips the streamer's camera Y-axis for a while.

🚫 !buy nojump 200 pts

Disables the streamer's jump for a while.

📦 !buy drop 150 pts

Makes the streamer drop whatever item they're currently holding.

Chat vote polls

Every so often (or whenever a mod runs !startpoll), the bot posts two chaos options in chat - free, no points needed. Just type 1 or 2 to vote. Whichever option gets the most votes happens automatically when the timer runs out.

Everyone

💰 !balance

Check how many points you've got saved up.

🎁 !give <username> <amount>

Moderators/broadcaster only - hands out points to a viewer (e.g. for a giveaway).

🗳️ !startpoll

Moderators/broadcaster only - starts a free chat vote poll on demand.

Every successful redemption gets a confirmation reply in chat, and shows up as an animated toast on stream if the streamer's running the OBS overlay.
