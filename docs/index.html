🌊 Waterpark Chaos Commands

WaterparkSimTwitchExpansion lets chat mess with the stream's Waterpark Simulator save in real time. Watching earns points automatically - spend them below to launch a guest into the air, sabotage the streamer's jump, and more. Commands marked Testing are still being verified live and might not work exactly as described yet.

How points work

💬 Just chatting +10 / 60s

Everyone active in chat earns points automatically, no commands needed.

🆕 Starting balance

The first time you're ever seen in chat: 250 pts normally, 500 pts if you follow the channel, or 1000 pts if you're a VIP or moderator - highest tier wins. One-time, doesn't change your balance later.

⭐ Subscribing / resubbing 500 × tier

Tier 1/2/3 (Prime counts as tier 1) - every new sub and every monthly resub.

🎁 Gifting a sub 500 × tier

Goes to whoever gifted, not the recipient - once per sub, so gifting 5 pays out 5 times.

💎 Cheering bits 1 pt / bit

1 point per bit cheered.

Everyone

💰 !balance

Check how many points you've got saved up.

📜 !waterparkcommands

Lists every !buy action and its point cost right in chat.

🎁 !give <username> <amount>

Moderators/broadcaster only - hands out points to a viewer (e.g. for a giveaway).

🗳️ !startpoll

Moderators/broadcaster only - starts a free chat vote (also runs on its own every so often). Just type 1 or 2 to vote, no points needed.
